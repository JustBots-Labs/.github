# Getting help

- **Questions and how-to:** start a discussion on the repository, or search existing issues.
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the bug form.
- **Security problems:** report privately to security@kelpgate.cloud or through GitHub; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Account, billing or service problems:** use the Kelpgate Console at https://console.kelpgate.cloud, or email support@kelpgate.cloud.

Include your version and, when you contact us about a scan, the `X-Request-Id` from the response. It lets us find the exact request.
